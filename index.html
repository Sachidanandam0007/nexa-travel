/ About

Capacity into revenue

I lead commercial strategy across airline cargo, global key accounts, outsourced capacity, procurement, freight forwarding and premium cargo verticals.

Most recently I led Turkish Cargo's UK commercial function, covering scheduled passenger belly capacity and freighter operations, from market strategy through to customer execution.

I am most effective where commercial transformation is needed: entering or rebuilding markets, sharpening customer segmentation, building premium cargo products and aligning commercial ambition with operational reality.

My PhD gives me a research-led view of competitive advantage and organisational change. In practice, I use it to build cargo strategies that are profitable and structurally sustainable.
